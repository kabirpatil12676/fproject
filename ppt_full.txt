1. 
Introduction
7/
30
/2026
ELEVATE: EDUCATOR-LED EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING PLATFORM
3
ΓÇó Evolution of Digital Learning:
Digital learning platforms have made education more accessible by enabling learners to access courses anytime and anywhere. However, most platforms primarily focus on content delivery rather than competency development.
ΓÇó Limitation of Existing Platforms:
Current Learning Management Systems (LMS) such as Moodle and online course platforms like Udemy emphasize videos, quizzes, and course completion but provide limited opportunities for experiential learning and continuous feedback.
ΓÇó Educator-Led Experiential Learning:
ELEVATE introduces a structured learning approach where educators guide learners through practical activities, reflection, validation, and continuous improvement instead of passive content consumption.
ΓÇó Holistic Student Development:
The platform integrates Growth Journeys, AI-assisted feedback, and Growth Portfolios to support technical, professional, and life-skill development through measurable learning outcomes.

2. Problems 
Identified
7/
30
/2026
EDUCATOR-LED EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING PLATFORM
4
1. Passive Content-Centric Learning
Learning is often limited to videos and quizzes with minimal practical application or real-world experience.
2. Lack of Structured Experiential Learning
Students have limited opportunities to learn through guided activities, reflection, and hands-on experiences.
3. Limited Personalized Feedback
Most platforms provide generic assessments without continuous mentoring or individualized guidance.
4. Absence of Evidence-Based Skill Assessment
Course completion certificates do not adequately demonstrate practical competencies or learner growth.
5. Poor Support for Holistic Student Development
Technical knowledge is prioritized, while communication, leadership, teamwork, and life skills receive limited attention.
To design and evaluate an educator-led experiential pedagogical model and personalized digital platform that supports life-skill development, professional readiness, and holistic student growth beyond conventional classroom learning.
